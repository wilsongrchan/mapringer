How to play

  1. Each day there's a new ring size and a target population.
  2. Move the map so the ring covers a place you think holds about that many people.
  3. Lock it in. You get one attempt, then the real count is revealed, along with the closest ring that would have hit the target.

Pick a map before you start: Easy (satellite with labels) scores ×0.8, Standard (light map, no roads) ×1, and Hard (just land, lakes and rivers) ×1.5.

Scoring is out of 100 on a log scale: near misses barely cost you, and big misses still score something. 2× too many (or too few) people scores about 81, 10× off about 42.
