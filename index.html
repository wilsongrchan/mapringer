How to play

Lock in ring 49,800 people · target 51,000 · 100 🎯
  1. Each day: one ring size, one target population.
  2. Pan the map to move the ring over a place you think fits.
  3. Lock it in. You get one try, then see the real count and the closest ring that would have hit it.

Maps: Hard (land, lakes, rivers) ×1.2 · Standard (no roads) ×1 · Easy (satellite) ×0.8. You start on Hard and can only switch to an easier map.

Scoring (out of 100): within 5% of the target is a perfect 100. After that it drops on a log scale: 2× too many (or too few) scores about 70, 10× off about 27.
